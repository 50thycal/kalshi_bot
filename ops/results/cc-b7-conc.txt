HOW TO READ THIS
  avg/outcome  distinct MARKETS this series held per contest. 1.00 means every
               position rode its own outcome; 8.66 means the average outcome carried
               8.66 contracts, which is one position at 8.66x size however the risk
               model counts it.
  %multi       share of contests holding more than one market. READ IT BESIDE the
               loss columns: at ~100% there is no single-market comparison group, so
               'all the losses came from multi-market contests' is TAUTOLOGICAL and
               says nothing about cause. A series with a MIX is the informative one.
  loss multi / single
               gross contest-level losses, split by whether the contest held more
               than one market. Concentration makes losses fat-tailed; nothing here
               shows it causes them.
  cross-series how many OTHER traded series share this one's event token — the
               XOS-000020 axis. `date?` means the token is only a date, so it
               collides with every date-keyed series and the figure is meaningless.

  A high number is EXPOSURE, not a verdict. The lever is the contest cap
  (docs/MMSELL_CORRELATION_CAP.md), not a ban: see docs/MMSELL_SERIES_APPROVAL_REVIEW.md.
