(1) HISTORY LOSS: a force-refresh replaces the branch wholesale, discarding ops/results/*.txt - the per-run files that exist precisely so concurrent producers can each read their own output - and can land on top of another session's in-flight ops/request.json, so a submitted command is silently replaced before the runner reads it. OBSERVED, without harm, on 2026-08-24: while this session was driving issue-command envelopes for XOS-000004 and XOS-000005, another session force-refreshed ops from a feature branch (commit message 'refresh from the taxonomy-repair branch').
