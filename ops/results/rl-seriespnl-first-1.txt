HOW TO READ THIS
  edge     be% - loss%, in percentage points. The ONE column comparable across
           series, because each is entered at a different premium. edge <= 0 means
           the cell is not paying for its tail.
  cnts     distinct CONTESTS — the honest independence denominator. One game carries
           a whole nested ladder and settles it against a seller at one instant, so
           `mkts` overstates n. Read c/trade against `cnts`, not against `n`.
  worst3%  share of a LOSING cell's damage from its three worst contests. High means
           a few catastrophic afternoons (a concentration problem, which the contest
           cap addresses); low means a broad negative drift (a selection problem,
           which it does not).

  A negative cell here is a PLACE TO LOOK, not a verdict. At this book's measured
  variance a single series needs n ~ 800 distinct markets before its CI excludes
  break-even (docs/MMSELL_ROADMAP.md §1); almost nothing on this page has that.
  Acting on a cell is a universe change to a running book, which under NEW_ONLY is
  a new epoch or version — never a config tweak, and never a side effect of a report.
