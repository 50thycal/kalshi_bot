Mention Market Listener

Your sketch: read each market's rules, listen to every live source, let an LLM predict the next words, and have the Kalshi bot trade on it. Can we build it, and should we?

Yes, it is buildable. Every box ran here on real inputs: Kalshi's own rules text, three real earnings-call recordings through a streaming speech recognizer, and the trade tape of a full live show.

No, don't build the full bot yet. When a word is said, someone usually sweeps the order book within one or two seconds. Others sell this exact tool. The CFTC now treats mention contracts as presumptively manipulable.

Next step: a no-orders probe over 10 broadcasts that answers the one question that decides it. Do we hear the word before the market moves?

Your sketch, box by box

Four boxes, four verdicts

Works

Mention rules

Prototype turns Kalshi market text into exact matching rules: plurals and possessives count, other inflections don't, "A / B" alternatives, "5+ times" counts. It warns instead of guessing when a clause is missing. 53 tests.

Hardest

Live sources

You called it. Speech-to-text is fast (14× real time on 2 CPU cores). The feed decides the race: an internet stream lags over-the-air TV by seconds, and Railway can't host an antenna.

Unproven

LLM predicts

The market already prices in-play decay better than a naive model. To add value, the LLM has to forecast the show's rundown better than the crowd does.

Gated

Kalshi bot

Must act on provisional hits, because confirmed text arrives 3 s later (median). Position limits and Experiment OS registration apply. Live money is a hard stop.

The LLM box, tested against the crowd

The market already knows how news shows work

A simple model says a word that hasn't been said gets steadily less likely as the show runs. The crowd moved faster than that: its price sat below the model at 70% of checkpoints. A story either leads the show or isn't coming. Trading the gap lost money on the YES side.

Is anyone else doing this?

Yes, in the open

  • Sonist sells live mention trading: audio or captions to a word-by-word transcript to IOC orders on Kalshi and Polymarket. Its claims are unverified and auto-trading is waitlist-only. listing
  • Open-source pipelines, small but real. polymarket-speech (livestream to Deepgram to Polymarket orders, with a kill switch) and kalshi-mentions (YouTube Live to Whisper to Kalshi paper trades; its notes say platform delay dominates).
  • Rules engines: mention-rules reproduces 2,306 settled Kalshi and Polymarket outcomes at 98.3%. Its authors tested 5 mention edges and falsified 4.
  • Research: arXiv 2602.21229 forecasts earnings-call mentions with LLMs, using the market price as a prior.
  • Paid feeds: Aiera and Benzinga sell live earnings-call transcripts.

Detecting a word is a commodity. An edge would have to come from a faster feed, flawless rule reading, or better forecasts of the words still to come.

Venue and regulation

The category is shrinking

  • CFTC staff advisory, Sep 22 2026 (Letter 26-27): mention contracts are presumptively manipulable. It expects position limits, restricted lists and surveillance during events. Non-binding, but limits would also cap a fast bot's size.
  • Kalshi paused sports mention markets around Aug 1 during a CFTC review.
  • On today's scan there are 0 open Trump mention markets, and all 455 open mention markets together traded 32,617 contracts in 24 hours.
  • The mention share of Kalshi's liquidity-incentive pool fell to about $3 a day.

Decision for you

Run the first-mover probe, or stop here

Ten broadcasts of one daily show, with the listener on the best feed we can get and no orders. Log when we detect each word, then compare with the moment the market jumps.

Pass

Our median detection comes at least 1 s before the market's halfway point on 6 or more of 10 said-word jumps.

Kill

We are later than the market on most jumps. The detection idea is then dead on measured evidence.

This needs a small listener service running during broadcasts, so it is your decision. Nothing has been started. Don't build the prediction half until an LLM rundown forecast beats the market's own decay on a pre-registered multi-show sample.

Limits. Market evidence comes from one show plus a replication. Jump speeds use 1-second trade timestamps, not order-book updates. Detection latency is the recognizer's own, without feed delay. The test calls are from 2020 and phone-quality. Testing against the mention-rules code was blocked by session permissions; its published rule table was used instead.
